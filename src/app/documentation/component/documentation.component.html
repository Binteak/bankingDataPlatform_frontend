Currently, in the context of Stress Test CCR there is no capacity to execute internal MtM stress test exercises with the required frequency, due to a set of methodological and computational limitations:

• Stress testing calculation environment -Murex- shares resources with production environments causing computational limitations.

• Lack of calculation engines in an automatic, agile and flexible way to meet the internal and regulatory stress tests exercises.

• High operational risk due to manual processes and a large range of different IT systems involved in the execution. Time and resources consuming

• There is only a specific methodology and process to meet EBA wide stress test biennial exercises, not covering other internal exercises.

To provide the capacity to cover other possible internal uses, a ST CCR Director Plan (2020-2023) has been defined. The first strategic initiative -Sensitivities- is focused on define and implement a homogeneous and robust stressed MtM estimation process, via sensitivities, aligned with industry practices and regulatory standards. This will allow to execute internal stress exercises in an agile way and with the adequate frequency (to comply with the regulatory requirement of the use of the internal CCR stress test) and also enable to challenge regulatory exercises required by supervisory bodies.

Sensitivities initiative has also successfully concluded during 2020 and includes the following phases:

I. Methodological definition for the calculation of stressed MtM proxy, with enough precision, for the projection of the stressed MtM by product based on the shocks applied by type of sensitivity.

II. Implementation of stressed MtM estimation process in a robust and integrated infrastructure framework (Stress test analytical node) allowing to increase IT systems capacity and computing times, avoiding the current high operational risk arisen from diversity input sources and manual processes and reducing time and resources dedication.

The scope of the initiative includes the perimeter of operations of Spain and UK, that due to the characteristics of the current process require to be estimated on Murex stress test environment. However, this methodology could be extrapolated in a similar way to the rest of the operations of other units, if appropriate.
